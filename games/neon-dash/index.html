How to play

Stay on your feet and keep moving. Jump over the ground barriers, leap over the hovering drones, and collect the glowing energy orbs along the way.

Runner tips

You get two jumps before you need to land. Use the second one to recover or reach higher orbs.
Energy orbs add bonus points. Keep an eye out for them above and between obstacles.
