How to play

Stay on the move across three tracks. Switch lanes to avoid trains, jump over low barriers, and slide under overhead gates. You have three hull points, so try not to take too many hits.

Runner tips

Trains block an entire lane. Barriers need a jump; low overhead gates need a slide.
Collect glowing energy orbs for bonus points. The metro speeds up the longer you survive.
