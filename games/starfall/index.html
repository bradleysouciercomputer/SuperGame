How to play

Move your ship left and right while your cannons handle the firing. Destroy every enemy in a wave before the next group arrives. Don't let enemies or their shots hit your fighter.

Upgrade your fighter

Blue shields absorb one hit. Gold power cells temporarily upgrade your cannons.
